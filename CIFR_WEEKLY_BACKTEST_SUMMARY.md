## 🔍 **KEY INSIGHTS**

### 1. **Different Stocks Need Different Strategies**

**HOOD (high volatility):**
- VegaEdge wins (-0.22% vs -7.52%)
- Keltner fails (catches falling knives)
- Volatility signals > Technical signals

**CIFR (range-bound):**
- Keltner wins (+0.21% vs -0.56%)
- VegaEdge overtrades (too many signals)
- Technical signals > Volatility signals

---

### 2. **Keltner Works When...**
✅ Stock is range-bound (bounces at levels)  
✅ Volatility is moderate (not extreme like HOOD)  
✅ Support/resistance levels hold  
✅ Fewer whipsaws  

❌ Keltner Fails When...
- Stock crashes hard (HOOD -81% then +1,900%)
- Volatility is extreme
- Falls through support levels
- No clear ranges

---

### 3. **VegaEdge Works When...**
✅ Extreme volatility (HOOD: $7→$148→$76)  
✅ IV spikes and crashes frequently  
✅ No clear technical patterns  
✅ Trend-following needed  

❌ VegaEdge Fails When...
- Stock is range-bound (CIFR)
- Volatility is stable
- Generates too many signals
- Low win rate + poor ratio

---

### 4. **Stock Characteristics Matter**

**CIFR vs HOOD:**
- CIFR: More stable, range-bound
- HOOD: Extreme volatility, trending
- CIFR: 190 weeks of data (longer history)
- HOOD: 167 weeks (less data)

**Implication:**
- Can't use one-size-fits-all strategy
- Need to classify stocks first
- Apply appropriate strategy

## 🎓 **LESSONS LEARNED**

### 1. **Stock Classification Required**

Before choosing strategy, classify stock:

**High Volatility Stocks (use VegaEdge):**
- HOOD, TSLA, GME, AMC
- Extreme IV spikes
- Trending/crashing behavior
- VegaEdge captures volatility edge

**Range-Bound Stocks (use Keltner):**
- CIFR, Utilities, Dividend stocks
- Respects technical levels
- Moderate volatility
- Keltner captures mean reversion

---
